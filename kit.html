DIT Notion Kit

A companion to DIT Media Calculator · not released yet

The calculator tells you how much footage you are about to make. This is where the rest of the job lives: what was actually shot, whether it is really backed up, where the drives physically are tonight, and whether the invoice was ever paid.

The part that matters

A card does not go green until the number of verified copies that job requires is actually ticked — and a card offloaded with no checksum never goes green at all.

⛔ DO NOT FORMAT — 3 cards on Nightshade

A005 · copy 2 not verified · B002 · no checksum method · B003 · copy 2 not verified

That view is the default screen of the whole kit. Everything below it is a tracker; this is the part that exists because formatting a card that was not really backed up is the mistake you only make once.

What is in it

  • Jobs — enquiry to paid, with day rate, kit rate and how many verified copies that production demands.
  • Shoot days — call, wrap, overtime, cameras rolling, TB in.
  • Cards & rolls — roll, card serial, size, each copy and its checksum, and the format gate above.
  • Drives — chain of custody. Who has it, where it went, whether it came back.
  • LUTs & CDLs — versions, and which look the DP actually approved.
  • Kit list — serials, insurance values, service dates, and the rental rate that feeds the invoice.
  • Invoices — issued, due, paid, days overdue.
  • Wrap report — one page per job to hand the production: day count, total TB, card register, incidents, drives handed over.

Where it does not work

Notion is useless with no signal, and sets are basements, stages, forests and tunnels.

So this is not built for tapping through during a take. Capture on set stays where it already is — paper, the card, the calculator. The kit is filled at wrap and in the truck, in one pass per day. A tool that is honest about where it does not work is worth more than one that is not.
