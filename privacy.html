Privacy Policy

DIT Media Calculator · Last updated 18 September 2026

Short version: The DIT Media Calculator app does not collect any personal data — no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no network calls of its own; your projects stay on your device. The only thing this website ever stores is an email address you choose to give us for product updates (details below).

Data we collect

None. The app does not gather, transmit or sell any personal or usage information.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or telemetry.
  • No advertising or third-party trackers.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera roll or microphone.

Your project data

Everything you enter — project details, camera setups, notes and any logo you add — is stored as ordinary files (.ditproj) in a folder you choose on your own device. You control where those files live. If you keep them in a cloud folder such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Google Drive, that syncing is handled by those services under their own privacy terms, not by this app.

PDF reports and sharing

Reports you export are created on your device and saved or shared only where you send them. The app does not upload them anywhere.

Email updates (this website)

The app itself still collects nothing. Separately, if you choose to enter your email address in the “Get product updates” form on this website, we store that address so we can email you occasionally about DIT Media Calculator.

  • What we store: your email address, the date you signed up and the exact consent wording you agreed to — nothing else. No name, no IP address, no open- or click-tracking.
  • Lawful basis: your consent, given by ticking the box on the form.
  • Who holds it: RedPixl s.r.o. is the data controller. The list lives on our own infrastructure (Cloudflare); no third-party mailing service is involved and no external sign-up script runs on the page.
  • Unsubscribe or erasure: ask us any time (see Contact) and we delete your address outright. Every email we send will include an unsubscribe link.

Children's privacy

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the support & community page.
